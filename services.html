Service 01

Kitchen Remodeling

From a cabinet-and-counter refresh to moving walls and rerouting plumbing. Most of our kitchen work falls into three tiers, and we will tell you honestly which one your house needs.

  • Full gut renovation, including layout changes
  • Cabinet replacement or refacing when the boxes are sound
  • Quartz, granite and solid surface countertop fabrication
  • Backsplash, sink, faucet and appliance install
  • Island builds, including adding power and plumbing
  • Recessed and under-cabinet lighting, plus electrical updates
  • Flooring, including tile, LVP and engineered hardwood
  • Permit handling where the scope requires it

Start a Kitchen Quote

Refresh

Same footprint. New counters, backsplash, sink, hardware and paint or refacing. The fastest option and the cheapest per visible change.

Typical timeline: 1–2 weeks

Replace

New cabinetry in the existing layout, plus counters, flooring, lighting and appliances. Plumbing and gas stay roughly where they are.

Typical timeline: 3–5 weeks

Reconfigure

Walls come out, the island moves, plumbing and electrical get rerouted. Permits and inspections apply. The biggest change and the longest schedule.

Typical timeline: 6–10 weeks

Service 02

Bathroom Remodeling

Bathrooms are the room where speed matters most, because you cannot easily live without one. We schedule consecutive working days and stage materials before demo so the crew is never waiting on a delivery.

  • Full bathroom rebuild down to the studs
  • Walk-in and curbless shower construction with proper waterproofing
  • Tub replacement and tub-to-shower conversion
  • Vanity, mirror, lighting and exhaust fan replacement
  • Floor and wall tile, including niches and benches
  • Toilet, valve and fixture replacement
  • Waterproofing and subfloor repair where rot is found
  • Accessible layouts for aging in place

Guest & hall baths

Small footprint, high impact. Usually the shortest schedule in the house and the easiest place to test a finish direction.

Primary suites

Double vanities, larger showers, separate water closets and the storage that never got planned properly the first time.

Accessible baths

Curbless entries, blocking behind the walls for grab bars, comfort-height fixtures and turning clearance that meets real needs.

Service 03

Specialty and single-scope work

Not every job is a full remodel. These are the focused projects we take on their own, usually on a much shorter schedule.

Tub-to-Shower Conversion

Remove an unused tub, rebuild the wet area, and install a low-threshold shower with a proper pan and waterproofing. One of the highest-return changes in an older home.

  • Full demo and haul-away
  • New pan, waterproofing and tile or surround
  • Valve, door and fixture install

Countertops & Cabinetry

Template, fabricate and install quartz, granite or solid surface. Cabinet refacing when the boxes are sound and full replacement when they are not.

  • Laser template and seam planning
  • Sink cutouts and edge profiles
  • Refacing with new doors and drawer fronts

Accessible & Aging-in-Place

Modifications that let someone stay in their home safely. We plan blocking behind the walls even when grab bars are not going in yet.

  • Curbless and low-threshold entries
  • Grab bar blocking and installation
  • Comfort-height fixtures and clear turning space

Our process

What happens between the first call and the last walkthrough

Consultation

About an hour at your house. Measurements, a look at existing conditions, and a straight conversation about budget before design starts.

Design & quote

Layout, finish selections and a line-item written price. Revisions are free. You owe nothing until you sign.

Order & schedule

Materials get ordered and staged before demo day. You get a start date, install days and a finish date in writing.

Install & close out

Consecutive working days, daily cleanup, then a walkthrough, punch list and written warranty handover.

Financing

0% interest for 18 months on approved credit, or fixed-rate terms out to 120 months on larger projects. Pre-approval takes a few minutes and does not change your quote.

Payment schedule

10% at signing, 40% when materials are delivered to your house, 40% at substantial completion, and the final 10% only after you sign off on the punch list.

Change orders

If we find something behind a wall, work on that area stops. You get photos, a written price, and the job does not resume until you approve it in writing. No verbal change orders.

Service questions

Details people ask about

Can I use my own materials or fixtures?
Yes, with one condition. We will install fixtures, tile and vanities you supply, but our 10 year workmanship warranty covers our labor only, not the product itself. You are responsible for ordering the right quantity, and we build in a 10% overage on tile. If a homeowner-supplied item arrives damaged or short, it becomes a schedule delay we cannot control.
Do I need to move out during the work?
Almost never. If you have a second bathroom, you can stay through a bath remodel without much disruption. For kitchens we set up a temporary station on day one, usually the fridge, microwave, coffee maker and a folding table in the dining room or garage, plus a utility sink where the plumbing allows. Most families stay put and eat out more than usual.
What happens if you find rot or a leak behind the wall?
This is the most common source of remodeling disputes, so here is exactly how we handle it. Work in that area stops. The project lead photographs what was found and texts it to you the same day. You get a written price for the repair within one business day. Nothing resumes until you approve it in writing. We never verbally agree to extra work and bill for it later. Rotted subfloor and old galvanized supply lines are the two things we find most often in Houston homes built before 1985.
Do you subcontract the work?
Demolition, framing, tile, cabinetry and finish carpentry are done by our own employees. Plumbing and electrical are performed by licensed trade partners we have worked with for over a decade, because Texas requires those trades to be licensed separately. They are insured, they are the same people every time, and they answer to our project lead rather than to you.
How far out are you booking?
As of August 2026 we are booking bathroom starts about 3 to 4 weeks out and kitchen starts about 6 to 8 weeks out. Consultations are available within the week. Cabinet lead times are the usual bottleneck on kitchens, so the sooner selections are locked, the sooner we can schedule.
What areas do you serve?
Greater Houston and surrounding counties. See the full service area list, and call us if your city is not on it, since we take some projects just outside the boundary.
