Common questions

Before you call

What does a remodel actually cost?
Bathrooms typically run $14,000 to $65,000 depending on size and whether the layout changes. Kitchens run $12,000 for a refresh up to $160,000 for a full reconfiguration. The variables are plumbing moves, cabinet line and countertop material. We publish our full ranges on the pricing section and give you a line-item written price after the in-home visit.
How long will my house be torn up?
A tub-to-shower conversion is 2 to 3 working days. A guest bathroom is 5 to 8. A primary bathroom is 2 to 3 weeks. Kitchens run 1 to 2 weeks for a refresh and 3 to 5 weeks for a full replacement, driven mostly by cabinet and countertop lead times. We order and stage everything before demo day so the crew is never sitting idle waiting on a delivery.
Do you handle permits?
Yes. We pull permits in the City of Houston and in Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery and Brazoria counties, and we schedule the inspections. Permits are included in your quoted price. Cosmetic work that does not move plumbing, gas or electrical usually does not require one, and we will tell you which category your project falls into at the consultation.
Is financing available?
Yes. We offer 0% interest for 18 months on approved credit, and fixed-rate terms out to 120 months for larger projects, through our lending partners. Pre-approval takes a few minutes and does not affect your quote. Financing is subject to credit approval, and terms are disclosed in writing before you sign anything.
What does the warranty cover?
Two separate warranties. Materials carry the manufacturer’s own warranty, which varies by product and is listed in your paperwork. Our labor and workmanship carry a 10 year written warranty, including waterproofing and tile installation. Both documents are handed over at the final walkthrough, not mailed later.
