Before you book

Quick answers

Is the consultation really free?
Yes. No charge, no deposit and no obligation to have us come out, measure and put a written line-item quote in your hands. Your quote is good for 30 days.
How long does the visit take?
About an hour for a single bathroom, longer for a kitchen or a whole-house scope. We would rather spend the time up front than get the estimate wrong.
Do I need to have decided on finishes first?
No. Bring inspiration photos if you have them, but the first visit is about measurements, existing conditions and budget. Finish selection comes after.
Can I just send photos instead?
Yes. Email photos to hello@superspeedremodel.com and we will tell you what category of project it is and a rough range before anyone comes out.

Would rather just talk to someone?

Call during business hours and a person answers. No phone tree, no callback queue.
